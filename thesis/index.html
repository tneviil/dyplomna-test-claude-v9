What does the service include?

01

Consultation and topic selection

we will help you choose a relevant research topic and agree on it with you. If you have a ready-made topic, we will carefully study the task and immediately start working

02

Drawing up an individual plan

We will prepare a detailed plan of the thesis (table of contents) and, if necessary, provide it to you for approval at the department. A properly formed plan guarantees a logical structure and full disclosure of the topic

03

Writing the theoretical part

We will collect and analyze modern scientific sources (textbooks, articles, monographs), reveal key concepts and concepts of your diploma topic.

04

Performing the practical part

We will conduct the necessary calculations, experiments or data analysis. It will contain our own research: cases, tables, figures, results of surveys or experiments - depending on the specifics of your specialty

05

Designing work according to standards

We will comply with all the requirements of your university: correct fonts and indents, design of the title page, table of contents, list of references, appendices, etc. Your diploma will be structured and look flawless.

How we work: stages of order fulfillment

1/ 5
  1. 1

    1. Submitting an application

    Leave a request on the website (via the order form) or contact us in a convenient messenger. You need to indicate the topic of the work, subject, page volume, desired percentage of uniqueness and deadline. Also provide methodological recommendations and teacher requirements, if any - this will help to accurately assess the task. Submitting an application does not oblige you to anything - this is just the first step to receiving a consultation and calculating the price.

  2. 2

    2. Order evaluation and confirmation

    We select the best author for the topic (all interested performers respond, and we choose the one who has the necessary qualifications and free time). Then the manager contacts you and informs you of the exact cost and deadline for the work. If everything suits you - we confirm the order and move on to the next step. (If not - we cancel without any questions).

  3. 3

    3. Writing a thesis

    The author carefully fulfills your order. At this stage, we maintain contact: the manager can clarify details or request additional materials (for example, an approved plan or source data for the practical part). You can also monitor the progress through your personal account or requests to the manager. If agreed, we will send you intermediate results (for example, a finished chapter) for review. After writing, all work undergoes an internal quality check and plagiarism check.

  4. 4

    4. Handover and payment

    When the thesis is ready, we will send you a fragment of the work for review or hold a presentation of the work. You will make sure that everything is done correctly. After that, you pay the remaining amount, and we send you the full text of the thesis with all attachments. Together with the work, you receive an anti-plagiarism report, as well as a presentation and speech for defense (if ordered).

  5. 5

    5. Warranty support and protection

    After receiving your diploma, you have time to review it yourself and show it to your supervisor. If you have any corrections, just send them to us, and the author will promptly correct everything. We will be in touch until the day of the defense to advise or correct any minor details, if necessary. As a result, you successfully defend your thesis and receive your degree! Don't forget to share the good news with us later 😃 and leave a review.
